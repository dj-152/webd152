The Origins of Pacific Trails Resort

A Call to the Wild

Our founders started Pacific Trails Resort after experiencing the crumbling high stress of working a regular 9-5 desk job. To help decompress, a suggestion from one of their coworkers was to go take a walk among nature in Big Sur.

After that following weekend, they founded Pacific Trails Resort to offer this journey back to mother nature to everyone.

Our Goal

Pacific Trails Resort's goal is to offer a rejuvenating retreat close to the beachfront. Our team is dedicated to assist our guests with all of their needs to make their stay worthwhile. We do our upmost best to provide each guest with the following accommodations listed below:

What We Offer

  • Affordable lodging with amenities
  • High quality meals from top rated chefs passionate about keeping our guest well fed.
  • Equipment for your aquatic adventure such as kayaks at the ready. Fresh towels provided for those relaxing at our whirlpool & outdoor heated pool.
  • Tours through the scenic trails of the local flora of Big Sur, lead by the finest nature experts we have on staff.
